# Privacy Policy — Bookmark: Save, AI Recall

**App:** Bookmark: Save, AI Recall  
**Developer:** appdeveloper-studios  
**Repository:** https://github.com/appdeveloper-studios/Bookmark  
**Effective date:** 2026-10-04

## 1. What this app is

Bookmark helps you save links, photos, notes, and documents so you can find them later. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and how you can control it.

## 2. Information we collect

### Account information (only if you sign in)

If you sign in, the app collects your **email address** so it can identify your account, sync your saves across devices, and let you sign in on a new device. If you use the app without signing in, no account information is collected.

### Content you save

The app stores the things you explicitly save: **links, titles, text, photos, and documents**. That content is yours. We process it only so the app can show it back to you, organize it, and — when you turn on the AI feature — help describe or summarize it.

### Technical and usage information

The app may collect information needed to keep it working, such as crash or error reports and basic usage signals. We do not track your browsing across other apps or websites.

## 3. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

- Let you save, search, organize, and recall the content you want to keep.
- Generate titles, summaries, or descriptions when you use the AI feature.
- Operate sign-in and sync when those features are turned on.
- Keep the app reliable and fix problems.

## 4. AI feature — third-party AI processing

When you use the AI feature, the **content you ask the app to understand** (for example a URL, a page, a photo, or a document) may be sent to a third-party AI service so the app can generate a title, summary, or description.

- This happens only when you use the feature, and only for the content you selected or the page the app is analyzing for you.
- We do not use your content for advertising, and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.
- You can avoid third-party AI processing entirely by turning the AI feature off. The rest of the app keeps working without it.

## 5. Sync, storage, and accounts

- **Without an account:** your saves live only on the device. If you delete the app or its local data, those device-only saves are removed.
- **With an account and sync on:** your saves, collections, and reminders are stored in our backend so they are available on your other signed-in devices.

## 6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information.

We only share information with others when:

- It is necessary to provide a feature you chose, such as AI processing for a link, photo, or document, or sync through our backend provider.
- A service provider needs it to help run the app (for example hosting or authentication), under agreements that limit how they can use it.
- We are required to do so by law.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including secure storage and transmission. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

## 8. Your choices and controls

You can:

- Use Bookmark without signing in and without turning on the AI feature.
- Sign in or stop signing in.
- Turn the AI feature off or on.
- Delete individual saves, collections, or reminders.
- Delete your account or clear local data, which removes data associated with the app on that device.

## 9. Children

Bookmark is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The revised policy will be posted in the app, and material changes will be communicated where appropriate.

## 11. Contact

If you want us to delete your account and associated data, you can request it
from within the app or by contacting us through the link below. When you make a
request, we delete the data tied to your account: your saves, collections,
reminders, and the content you saved in them, plus your account information, such
as your email address. We do not keep that data after the request is processed,
except where we are required to retain it by law. If you used a third-party AI
feature, some information may have been sent to that provider; we do not control
retention on their side. Please allow a reasonable time for the request to be
processed.

To request account deletion, use this link:
https://github.com/appdeveloper-studios/Bookmark/issues/new?template=account-deletion.md

To request that we delete personal data from any third-party service we use, use
the same link and tell us which service.
