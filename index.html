Common questions

How do I save something?

In any app, tap Share and choose Bookmark. You can also copy a link and tap + in Bookmark.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Bookmark Pro, and choose Cancel. If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the Bookmark paywall and tap Restore.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open Profile, then Account, then Delete account.

How do I export my saves?

Open Profile, then Privacy and data, then Export my library.
