Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What Bookmark is

Bookmark is a personal library for links, notes, photos, videos and documents you choose to save. Your saves live on your device.

What stays on your device

Your saves, notes, tags, shelves, reminders and settings are stored on your device. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

AI features — only with your permission

If you allow it, Bookmark sends what you save — the link, the page title and text, your note, a photo, a cover picture or a short video clip — through our server to Google’s Gemini service so it can name, summarize and tag your save. This happens only after you give permission, and only for items you save. Our server only passes the request on and does not keep it. You can turn it off at any time in Profile → Privacy and data → AI, and Bookmark keeps working without it. Google processes this data under its own terms.

Link previews

To show a preview, your device fetches the web page you saved directly from that website, like opening it in a browser.

Cover pictures for links

When you save a link, Bookmark looks up its cover picture. For some sites (such as YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Snapchat, Amazon and X) the link address — and nothing else — is sent to that site’s own public preview service, or to a link-preview service (microlink.io, or fxtwitter for X posts), so it can return the cover. Your notes, photos and other saves are never sent for this.

Your account (optional)

You can use Bookmark without an account. If you sign in with Apple, Google or an email link, we store your sign-in identifier and email address with our authentication provider, Supabase, only to keep you signed in. If you use Sign in with Apple’s private email option, we only see Apple’s relay address.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. Bookmark checks whether Pro is active on your device. To keep AI use fair, the app sends Apple’s signed proof of your subscription (a token with no payment details) to our server, which counts requests per day. We never see your payment details.

Notifications, camera and microphone

Reminders use local notifications you allow in iOS Settings. The camera is used only when you choose Snap. The microphone and Apple speech recognition are used only while you tap the mic to search by voice.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can export your library at any time (Profile → Privacy and data). To delete everything, go to Profile → Account → Delete account (or Delete all data if you are not signed in). This erases your saves on this device and deletes your account. You can also withdraw AI permission at any time.

Retention

Saves stay on your device until you delete them. Items in Recently deleted are removed after 30 days. Account data is deleted when you delete your account.

Contact

You can reach us through the Support link on Bookmark’s App Store page.

What is sent

With your permission, the link, page title and text, your note, the photo, cover picture or short video clip of a save is sent through our server to Google’s Gemini service to write a name, a short summary and tags. Searching your saves, including in plain words, happens on your device and sends nothing.

What is not sent

Your photos and files (except the one being named), your contacts, your location, and your payment details are never sent. Nothing is sent when AI is off.

Your control

Turn AI on or off any time in Profile → Privacy and data. Turning it off keeps your existing saves exactly as they are.

Accuracy

AI output can be wrong. Everything it writes can be edited, and your own edits are kept.
